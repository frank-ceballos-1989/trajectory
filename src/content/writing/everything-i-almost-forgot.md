I feel very grateful for where I am now, and for everyone who helped me get here. I have never thanked them directly. So this is where I start: in writing, one person at a time. If any of you ever find this page, this is your thank-you note.

## Dr. Zhao

Dr. Zhao was my graduate advisor at the University of Kansas. We met in February 2012, when I went to visit. I was impressed by the facilities, by the graduate students I met, and above all by Dr. Zhao's friendliness and kindness. I decided on the flight back to Brownsville from Lawrence that I wanted to work for him.

I never thanked him properly. Well, I did, but I am an awkward person. He was the first person to call me Dr. Ceballos, right after my PhD defense. That was the most nerve-wracking moment of my life. Nothing has put that much pressure on me, before or since: five professors I had chosen sat there and discussed my work, my presentation and my performance. But this note is about Dr. Zhao and why I thank him.

**Thank you for believing in me.** You supported me for five years of my life.

**Thank you for the best years.** I lived them in Lawrence, Kansas, because of you.

**Thank you for the purpose.** You gave me reasons to work hard. My goal was clear, the path was clear, and your guidance carried me through it. Without you, I would never have reached this place.

**Thank you for the kindness.** Without your friendliness and kindness, I would have given up.

I am proud to see you carry the lab forward and give many others the same opportunity you gave me. Thank you. I will never forget you, and I will visit you again. It is about time I went back to Lawrence, a town that doesn't know me anymore, but one I can't forget.

## Peymon

Peymon and I worked side by side in the ultrafast laser spectroscopy lab at the University of Kansas. I spent time teaching him the lab and helping him set up his first experiments. What I never said is how much he gave back.

**Thank you for the energy.** Graduate school wears you down, and you gave me the energy to keep working.

**Thank you for sharing your culture with me.** It made me a richer person, and I am still glad of it.

**Thank you for the soccer.** You scored on every assist I gave you. I will never forget the one I crossed straight onto your head, and the header that followed.

**Thank you for what came after the games.** We spent the week being beaten up by the lab, by graduate school and by life. Then we played, and then we talked. I still cherish those conversations.

I am proud of the time we shared, Peymon. Thank you.
